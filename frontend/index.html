📚 Complete Recycling Guide

Ask AI about any item — get instant Chennai-specific disposal guidance.

♻️
Plastics & Bottles
Most plastic containers and bottles can be recycled

✅ Accepted:

  • Water bottles
  • Milk jugs
  • Detergent bottles

❌ Not Accepted:

  • Plastic bags
  • Styrofoam
📄
Paper & Cardboard
Clean paper and cardboard items

✅ Accepted:

  • Newspapers
  • Cardboard boxes
  • Office paper

❌ Not Accepted:

  • Wax-coated paper
  • Greasy pizza boxes
🍾
Glass & Metal
Bottles, jars, cans, and metal items

✅ Accepted:

  • Glass bottles
  • Aluminum cans
  • Steel cans

❌ Not Accepted:

  • Broken glass
  • Ceramics
🌿
Organic Waste
Food scraps and yard waste for composting

✅ Accepted:

  • Fruit & vegetable scraps
  • Coffee grounds
  • Eggshells

❌ Not Accepted:

  • Meat & dairy
  • Pet waste
🔋
Electronics & Batteries
E-waste requires special handling

✅ E-waste Centers:

  • Smartphones
  • Batteries
  • Cables & chargers

⚠️ Special Handling:

  • Remove batteries first
  • Wipe device data
⚠️
Hazardous Waste
Requires special disposal facilities

⚠️ Hazardous Items:

  • Paint & chemicals
  • Motor oil
  • Pesticides

📍 Disposal:

  • Take to hazardous waste facility
  • Never pour down drain
